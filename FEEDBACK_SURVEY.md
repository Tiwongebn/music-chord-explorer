## Quick Overview (Before You Start)

The Music Chord Explorer interface has been redesigned to:
- **Explore Tab**: 2-column layout (controls on left, visualization on right)
- **Progressions Tab**: Controls grouped by function (Harmony Selection in purple, Playback & Groove in blue)
- **Chord Lab Tab**: No changes

**Please spend 5-10 minutes exploring the app and then answer the questions below.**
