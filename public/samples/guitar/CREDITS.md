# Guitar samples

Acoustic guitar notes (12 of the 38 in the original set, MP3) from
[tonejs-instruments](https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments)
by Nicholas Brosowsky and contributors. The repository README lists
the samples as CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)
and its `sample-source-info.txt` credits the acoustic guitar to the
University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios (https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/).

Distributed here unmodified apart from keeping a subset of the files.
Code in that repository is MIT licensed.
